# Designeer design inspiration

## Know what Designeer covers

- Treat Designeer as a hand-reviewed catalogue for designers and developers.

- Cover these angles when you brief a search: interface craft, design systems, component libraries, AI tools, inspiration, and portfolios from independent creators and studios.

- Browse the public catalogue without an account. Skip saving favourites and folders unless the user asks to save, because those need an account.

- Never guess a category or detail page URL. Take every URL from the site navigation, a search result, or a page you opened.

## Find references for a visual direction

- Ask for the direction when it is missing: the page or product type, the mood or style, and anything it must not look like.

- Turn the direction into one or two angles from the coverage list, such as motion or typography, before you search.

- Start with `browser.open` on https://designeer.xyz/ for a quick look at the current picks.

- Search the web with `browser.search` for the angle plus designeer.xyz when the homepage text is too thin to choose from.

- Open each promising find with `browser.open` and read its description, author, and original source link.

- Use `browser.browse` when a page needs scrolling, filtering, or interaction, because the text fetch shows only part of a JavaScript heavy page.

- Pick 3 to 5 references that differ in layout, color, motion, or type, and write one line per pick on what to borrow and what to avoid.

## Turn references into usable guidance

- Summarize the shared traits of the picks as 3 to 5 concrete rules, such as color restraint, type scale, spacing rhythm, or motion speed.

- Name what does not fit the direction and leave it out, instead of averaging every pick into a vague mood.

- Present each pick with its name as a link, one line on why it fits, and the rule it supports.

- Link to the original source instead of copying images or artwork into a deliverable, unless the license clearly allows reuse.

## Websites

- [Designeer](https://designeer.xyz/) for hand-reviewed design inspiration, UI resources, tools, and portfolios. Carry the direction and the angles into the browser task and verify each pick at its original source.

## Alternatives for better results

- Broaden one angle at a time when a narrow direction returns too few picks, such as dropping the industry while keeping the motion style, instead of falling back to a generic image search.

- Search a screen library by flow or pattern when the user wants real product screens rather than portfolio pieces.
